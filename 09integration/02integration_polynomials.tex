\begin{exercise}{Integration af polynomier 1-1}

Bestem det ubestemte integral $\int (3x^2 + 4x + 5) \, dx$.

\answerbox{x^3 + 2x^2 + 5x + C}

\hint

Integrer hvert led separat.

\hint

Regel: $\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$ for $n \neq -1$

\hint

For $3x^2$:
\[
\int 3x^2 \, dx = 3 \cdot \frac{x^{2+1}}{2+1} = 3 \cdot \frac{x^3}{3} = x^3 
\]

\hint

For $4x$:
\[
\int 4x \, dx = 4 \cdot \frac{x^{1+1}}{1+1} = 4 \cdot \frac{x^2}{2} = 2x^2 
\]

\hint

For $5$:
\[
\int 5 \, dx = 5x 
\]

\hint

Sammt alle led og tilføj integrationskonstanten $C$:
\[
x^3 + 2x^2 + 5x + C
\]

\end{exercise}

\begin{exercise}{Integration af polynomier 1-2}

Bestem det ubestemte integral $\int (2x^3 - x^2 + 3x - 7) \, dx$.

\answerbox{\frac{1}{2}x^4 - \frac{1}{3}x^3 + \frac{3}{2}x^2 - 7x + C}

\hint

Integrer hvert led separat:

\hint
\[
\int 2x^3 \, dx = 2 \cdot \frac{x^4}{4} = \frac{1}{2}x^4
\]

\hint
\[
\int -x^2 \, dx = -\frac{x^3}{3}
\]

\hint
\[
\int 3x \, dx = \frac{3x^2}{2}
\]

\hint
\[
\int -7 \, dx = -7x
\]

\hint

Sammt alle led med integrationskonstanten $C$:
\[
\frac{1}{2}x^4 - \frac{1}{3}x^3 + \frac{3}{2}x^2 - 7x + C
\]

\end{exercise}

\begin{exercise}{Integration af polynomier 1-3}

Bestem det ubestemte integral $\int (x^4 + 5) \, dx$.

\answerbox{\frac{1}{5}x^5 + 5x + C}

\hint

Integrer hvert led:

\hint
\[
\int x^4 \, dx = \frac{x^5}{5}, \quad \int 5 \, dx = 5x
\]

\hint

Sammt med integrationskonstanten:
\[
\frac{1}{5}x^5 + 5x + C
\]

\end{exercise}

\begin{exercise}{Integration af polynomier 1-4}

Bestem det ubestemte integral $\int (6x^5 - 2x^3 + x) \, dx$.

\answerbox{x^6 - \frac{1}{2}x^4 + \frac{1}{2}x^2 + C}

\hint

Integrer hvert led med reglen $\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$:

\hint
\[
\int 6x^5 \, dx = 6 \cdot \frac{x^6}{6} = x^6
\]

\hint
\[
\int -2x^3 \, dx = -2 \cdot \frac{x^4}{4} = -\frac{1}{2}x^4
\]

\hint
\[
\int x \, dx = \frac{x^2}{2}
\]

\hint

Sammt alle led:
\[
x^6 - \frac{1}{2}x^4 + \frac{1}{2}x^2 + C
\]

\end{exercise}
